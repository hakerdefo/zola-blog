If anyone has spent any time on the Indian side of the internet lately, they have most likely heard one of the many versions of this track going viral. It’s the track flooding Instagram Reels, trending on YouTube, and playing continuously in your head if you are a music lover. But this Saraiki folk tune, "Chhalre chhalre waal" goes way deeper than your average viral groove. My friend P****i spent quite a bit of her time transcribing and transcreating this track so non-Saraiki speakers can finally experience the lyrical depth behind the song. I must confess here that it's not a literal translation but her personal interpretation, so if you disagree with her take, feel free to email your version.
